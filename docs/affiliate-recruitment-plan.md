# Affiliate Recruitment Plan — InternsMarket

**Goal:** 50+ active affiliates by end of Month 2
**Target niche:** AI tools, SaaS productivity, developer tools, no-code, startup ops
**Offer:** 25% recurring commission, 12-month duration, tiered to 40%

---

## Phase 1 — Seed with Micro-Influencers (Week 1–2)

**Goal:** 10–20 signed affiliates. Prove conversion rate. Collect testimonials.

**Why micro-influencers first:** Higher engagement, lower cost, faster to say yes, authentic recommendations.

### Target Profile
- Audience: 1K–50K followers/subscribers
- Niche: AI tools, productivity, dev tools, no-code/automation
- Platform: YouTube, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, personal blog

### Sourcing Channels
- YouTube search: "AI tools for developers", "best AI productivity tools 2025"
- Twitter/X: search `#AItools`, `#devtools`, `#productivity` — find accounts with 2K–30K followers
- LinkedIn: search "AI tools" content in past 30 days, filter by engagement
- Newsletter databases: Substack (search AI/productivity category), Beehiiv discovery

### Vetting Criteria

| Criterion | Minimum Threshold |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Audience niche alignment | ≥60% AI/tech/productivity |
| Fake follower rate | <15% (check via HypeAuditor free tier) |
| Engagement rate | ≥2% (micro), ≥0.5% (10K+) |
| Brand safety | No controversy in last 12 months |
| FTC compliance | At least 1 prior sponsored post with proper disclosure |
| No competing partnerships | No active AI intern / AI assistant SaaS deals |

## Phase 2 — Review Sites, Directories & Newsletters (Week 3–4)

**Goal:** 30–50 total affiliates. Focus on SEO/organic traffic sources.

### Target Channels

| Channel | Examples | Approach |
|---------|----------|----------|
| SaaS review sites | G2, Capterra, ProductHunt | Submit product listing; contact top reviewers |
| AI tool directories | Futurepedia, There's An AI For That, TopAI.tools | Submit listing; negotiate affiliate partnership |
| Dev newsletters | TLDR, Pointer.io, Hacker Newsletter | Sponsored mention + affiliate deal |
| AI newsletters | The Rundown AI, Ben's Bites, The Neuron | Paid + affiliate hybrid |
| Indie hacker communities | IH, r/SaaS, r/SideProject | Community posts + affiliate offer |

### Outreach Approach for Publications

- Email newsletter owners directly with affiliate + sponsored deal combo
- Offer: 1 free sponsored mention + ongoing 25% affiliate commission
- Provide ready-to-send copy (reduces their effort to zero)
- Track via UTM + unique coupon code per publication

### Week 3–4 Success Metrics

- [ ] 5+ directory listings live
- [ ] 3+ newsletter mentions booked
- [ ] Total affiliates ≥30
- [ ] At least 3 conversions from affiliate traffic

## Phase 3 — Scale to 50+ + Add Agency Partners (Month 2)

**Goal:** 50+ affiliates, first agency partner, measurable affiliate-driven MRR.

### Agency Partner Model

Target: marketing agencies, dev consultancies, and freelancers who manage SaaS tools for clients.

Offer:
- 20% commission on all client accounts they manage under InternsMarket
- Dedicated account manager
- White-label option (review separately)
- Volume bonus: agency-level Platinum (40%) at 30+ client subscriptions

Sourcing:
- LinkedIn search "AI agency", "SaaS consultant", "automation agency"
- Reach out to Zapier/Make certified partners
- Post in agency Slack communities

### KOL Activation (Month 2)

Target 2–3 larger creators (10K–100K) for hybrid deal:
- $500–$1,000 flat fee for dedicated review video/post
- Plus standard affiliate commission ongoing
- Requires: 6-month minimum partnership term

### Month 2 Success Metrics

- [ ] 50+ total affiliates
- [ ] 1+ agency partner
- [ ] Affiliate-driven MRR ≥ $500
- [ ] Avg affiliate conversion rate tracked and benchmarked
- [ ] Fraud rate <2%

---

## Fraud Controls During Recruitment

- Verify affiliate's audience is real before approval (manual or HypeAuditor)
- 60-day commission hold — prevents hit-and-run fraud
- Flag: conversion rate >20% above program average → manual review
- Block: same IP/device creating multiple affiliate accounts
- Monthly audit: top 20% earners reviewed for anomalies
