# InternsMarket — Marketing Overview

---

## Executive Summary

InternsMarket is a SaaS platform selling AI intern personas at **$29.99/intern/month** with a **3-day free trial**. Post-pivot from CLI-only to cloud-hosted SaaS. Goals: brand awareness + trial→paid conversion. 11 unique AI interns with personality-first design.

**Launch Timeline:** Q2 2026 (Mar–May) | **Budget:** $0–500/mo (bootstrap, organic focus)

## Target Audience Profiles

### Primary Persona 1: Solopreneurs & Founders
- **Age:** 30–45
- **Income:** $50K–$150K+
- **Pain:** Too much shallow AI tool juggling, want real collaborators
- **Motivation:** Hire "teammates" without salary overhead
- **Example:** Founder automating customer support, content, analytics

### Primary Persona 2: Small Dev Teams
- **Age:** 25–40
- **Role:** Engineers, tech leads
- **Pain:** Code review tedious, need specialized AI for testing/docs
- **Motivation:** Specialized agents + no prompt engineering
- **Example:** Startup team wants Code Review intern for PRs

### Primary Persona 3: Content Creators & Agencies
- **Age:** 28–50
- **Income:** $30K–$200K+ (varies)
- **Pain:** Hiring content writers expensive, AI tools lack personality
- **Motivation:** Consistent AI teammate for blog, social, campaigns
- **Example:** Agency with Content Marketing + Social Media interns

**Geographic:** Global, English-speaking markets first. Secondary: European SaaS community.

## Channel Strategy (Bootstrap $0–500/mo Budget)

### Primary Channels

#### 1. Twitter/X (Organic)
- **Posting:** 3–5 tweets/week showcasing intern personalities
- **Content:** Use case stories, intern skill spotlights, community wins
- **Engagement:** Reply to AI community tweets, join conversations
- **Hashtags:** `#AIagents #SaaS #productivity #BuildInPublic`
- **Owned Account:** [@internsmkt](https://twitter.com) (to create)

#### 2. Discord Community (Owned)
- **Purpose:** User community, support, feedback loops
- **Channels:** `#introductions`, `#intern-showcases`, `#feature-requests`, `#bugs`
- **Activity:** Weekly updates, AMAs with founders
- **Target:** 500+ members by Q2 end

#### 3. Blog / SEO Content
- **2–3 posts/month:** Intern use cases, AI agent comparisons, tips
- **Topics:**
  - "How to Hire an AI Content Marketer for $30/mo"
  - "Personality-First AI: Why Tone Matters"
  - "InternsMarket vs CrewAI vs Lindy.ai"
- **Link to:** Landing page, referral program

### Secondary Channels

#### 4. Product Hunt (Launch)
- **Timing:** End of Month 2 (May 2026)
- **Teaser Campaign:** 2–3 weeks on Twitter + Discord
- **Pitch:** Personality-first AI agents, 3-day free trial
- **Target:** Top 10 on day 1, 500+ upvotes

#### 5. Reddit & HN
- **Subreddits:** r/artificial, r/startups, r/productivity, r/SideProject
- **Policy:** Authentic engagement, no spam
- **HN:** Only post if story is genuinely interesting (not forced)

#### 6. Referral Program (In-App)
- **Commission:** 20% recurring (user pays $29.99, referrer gets $6/mo)
- **Link:** Share unique referral code
- **Marketing:** "Earn $6/mo per referred user"
- **Phase:** Soft launch Q2 (May), full push Q3
