# Referral vs Affiliate Program — InternsMarket Comparison

---

## Side-by-Side Comparison

| Dimension | Referral Program | Affiliate Program |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Who participates** | Existing customers | External creators, bloggers, agencies |
| **Commission (base)** | 20% recurring, 12 months | 25% recurring, 12 months |
| **Top tier commission** | 40% (15+ conversions/mo) | 40% (30+ conversions/mo) |
| **Referee benefit** | 7-day trial (vs 3-day standard) | 7-day trial via affiliate link |
| **Cookie window** | 30 days | 30 days |
| **Min payout** | $50 | $50 |
| **Hold period** | 7-day clawback window | 60-day hold |
| **Application required** | No — auto-available to all customers | Yes — review + approval |
| **Creative assets** | Share widget, email, social | Full kit: banners, video, swipes |
| **Dashboard** | Customer account dashboard | Dedicated affiliate portal |
| **Payment methods** | PayPal or account credit | PayPal, Wise |
| **Fraud risk** | Low (existing customers, verified) | Medium (external, unknown) |
| **Traffic source** | Word-of-mouth, peer trust | Content, SEO, social, ads |
| **Avg conversion rate** | Higher (personal recommendation) | Lower (cold audience) |
| **Volume potential** | Lower (limited customer base) | Higher (unlimited reach) |
| **Setup complexity** | Low | Medium |
| **Ongoing management** | Low | Medium-High |

---

## When to Use Which

### Use Referral When:
- Customer base exists (even 50+ users is enough to seed)
- Want organic, low-cost growth from trusted advocates
- Limited bandwidth to manage external partnerships
- Early stage — need to validate product before scaling acquisition

### Use Affiliate When:
- Referral program is running and you want to scale reach
- Content creators in your niche have audiences you can't reach organically
- You can dedicate someone to affiliate management
- CAC from paid channels is too high and you need performance-based alternatives

---

## Can They Coexist?

Yes — they serve different audiences and shouldn't compete.

| | Referral | Affiliate |
|-|----------|-----------|
| Who | Existing customers | External creators |
| Motivation | Share something they love | Monetize their audience |
| Link type | Personal referral code | Affiliate tracking link |
| Overlap risk | Low — separate signup flows | Low |

**One person can be both:** An existing customer who is also a content creator can participate in both programs simultaneously — they'd use their referral code for personal shares and affiliate link for content.

---

## Priority Recommendation for InternsMarket

### Launch Order: Referral First, Affiliate in Month 2

**Month 1 — Launch Referral Program**

Reasons:
1. Simpler to build — no application/review flow needed
2. Lower fraud risk — customers are verified, payment methods on file
3. Faster to revenue — existing customers share immediately
4. Validates demand before investing in affiliate infrastructure
5. Product-market fit signal — if customers don't refer, affiliate won't convert either

**Month 2 — Launch Affiliate Program**

Reasons:
1. Referral data proves the product converts → affiliates can trust the offer
2. Commission rates are justified by volume affiliates bring
3. Asset creation, dashboard, and compliance tooling built properly

## Budget Allocation Suggestion

| Program | Month 1 | Month 2 | Month 3+ |
|---------|---------|---------|----------|
| Referral (commissions) | $0–$200 | $200–$600 | Scale with MRR |
| Affiliate (ops + commissions) | $0 | $400–$700 | $1,000–$3,000 |
| Affiliate (KOL flat fees) | $0 | $0–$1,000 | $1,000–$5,000 |

Referral commissions are purely performance-based — zero fixed cost.
Affiliate costs include outreach tools, asset creation, and flat-fee KOL deals.

---

## Risk Comparison

| Risk | Referral | Affiliate |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Self-referral fraud | Low (same-domain block) | Medium |
| Fake lead injection | Very low | Medium |
| Commission clawback disputes | Low | Medium |
| Brand misrepresentation | Very low | Medium (external control) |
| FTC compliance issues | Very low | Medium (must enforce disclosure) |
| Program abandonment (low ROI) | Low | Medium |
| **Overall risk** | **Low** | **Medium** |

**Mitigation for affiliate:** 60-day hold, fraud scoring, manual approval, clear TOS.

---

## TL;DR

- Launch referral in Month 1. Low risk, low cost, high trust.
- Launch affiliate in Month 2. Higher reach, more management, more reward.
- Both programs together maximize CAC efficiency across warm (referral) and cold (affiliate) channels.
- Neither replaces the other — they stack.
