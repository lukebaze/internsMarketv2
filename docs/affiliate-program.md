# InternsMarket Affiliate Program

**Target:** Content creators, agencies, tech bloggers, AI/productivity influencers
**Model:** Recurring commission — affiliates earn as long as referred customers stay
**Pricing context:** $29.99/intern/month, 3-day free trial, 11 AI interns

---

## Program Overview

Affiliates promote InternsMarket to their audiences and earn a percentage of every subscription payment for 12 months. Unlike the referral program (for existing customers), this program targets external creators who can drive volume.

**Core pitch:** Promote once. Earn monthly. For a year.

---

## Commission Structure

### Tiered by Monthly Conversions

| Tier | Monthly Conversions | Commission | Monthly earn (10 refs × 2 interns) |
|------|---------------------|------------|-------------------------------------|
| Bronze | Default | 25% | $149.95 |
| Silver | 5+ | 30% | $179.94 |
| Gold | 15+ | 35% | $209.93 |
| Platinum | 30+ | 40% | $239.92 |

**Why higher than referral (20% base)?** Affiliates drive external traffic, invest in content creation, and typically convert at lower rates — the premium compensates for effort and volume.

### How Commission Works

- 25% of each referred subscriber's monthly payment
- Per intern: $29.99 × 25% = **$7.50/intern/month**
- Duration: 12 months from referee's first paid subscription
- Applies to all interns a referred customer subscribes to
- Tier re-evaluated monthly; 1-month grace period before downgrade

**Example — Silver tier (5+ conversions):**
```
15 active referrals × avg 2 interns × $29.99 = $899.70 referred MRR
30% commission = $269.91/month
Annual = $3,238.92
```

## Compliance

### FTC Disclosure Requirements

Affiliates MUST:
- Disclose paid partnership in ALL promotional content
- Use clear language: "This is a sponsored post" or "Affiliate link — I earn a commission"
- Include disclosure before affiliate link, not buried at the bottom
- Follow platform-specific rules (YouTube info card, Instagram #ad, etc.)

Failure to disclose = immediate program suspension.

### Brand Guidelines

**DO:**
- Use provided assets and approved copy
- Share honest reviews, including limitations
- Link to official landing page

**DO NOT:**
- Claim features that don't exist
- Compare to competitors without facts
- Use InternsMarket logo in unauthorized ways
- Run paid ads targeting "InternsMarket" branded keywords

### Prohibited Practices

- Self-referral or fake account creation
- Cookie stuffing or toolbar-based attribution manipulation
- Bidding on "InternsMarket" or branded keyword variations in paid search
- Spam or unsolicited cold outreach on our behalf
- Coupons on third-party discount sites without prior approval

| Currency | USD only |

**Why 60-day hold?** Matches refund/chargeback window. Commissions only paid after the referred customer's subscription is confirmed stable.

**Clawback policy:** Commission reversed if referred customer cancels or charges back within 7 days of payment.
