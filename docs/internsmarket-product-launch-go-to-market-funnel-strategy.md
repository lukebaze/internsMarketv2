## 2. Funnel Stages

### Stage 1 — AWARENESS (Top of Funnel)

| Source | Content Type | Frequency | Target Metric |
|--------|-------------|-----------|--------------|
| Twitter/X | Intern personality threads, "Day in the Life" | 3-5x/week | 500K impressions/mo by M3 |
| Discord | Community posts, user showcases | Daily | 500 members by M3 |
| Product Hunt | Launch page + upvote campaign | Week 4 | 500+ upvotes, top 10 |
| Reddit (r/artificial, r/startups) | Authentic use case posts | 2x/month | 200 clicks/post |
| Hacker News | Show HN post (genuine story angle) | 1x (Week 4) | 100+ points |

**M3 Target:** 10,000 unique visitors/month | **M1 Target:** 2,000 visitors

### Stage 2 — INTEREST (Landing Page)

| Element | Description | Target |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| Hero | "Hire AI Teammates With Real Personality" + 3-day trial CTA | <3s above fold |
| Intern Gallery | Scrollable catalog with 11 intern cards | 20% scroll rate |
| Social Proof | Beta user count, testimonials, use cases | >3 testimonials at launch |
| Pricing | $29.99 / $79.99 / custom tiers visible | Pricing seen by 40% |

**Key Metric:** Gallery scroll rate 20%, intern card clicks 15%

### Stage 3 — CONSIDERATION (Intern Detail Overlay)

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Overlay content | Name, role, skills list, personality traits, sample outputs |
| Demo | 1 example task output per intern |
| CTA | "HIRE [NAME] — $29.99/MO" + "Start 3-Day Trial" |
| Trust signals | "No credit card. Cancel anytime." |

**Key Metric:** 30% of gallery browsers open overlay. 20% click CTA.

### Stage 4 — TRIAL (Conversion Entry)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| Signup | Email only, no CC, 3-day trial |
| Onboarding | Pick first intern → guided first task |
| Activation prompt | Auto-prompt to "Try a task in 60 seconds" |

**Target:** 10% of overlay visitors sign up. M1: 60 trials. M3: 250 trials/mo.

### Stage 5 — ACTIVATION (Aha Moment)

| Trigger | Definition | Target |
|---------|------------|--------|
| First task complete | Intern delivers first output within 24h | 60% of trialists |
| Engagement signal | 3+ tasks completed in trial | 30% of trialists |

**Tactic:** Onboarding email at T+2h if no task started. Activation nudge at T+1 day.

### Stage 6 — CONVERSION (Paid)

| Touchpoint | Timing | Message |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Trial expiry warning | Day 2 of trial | "Your trial ends tomorrow — keep [intern name]" |
| Expiry email | Day 3 (expiry) | Personalized recap of value delivered |
| Post-expiry offer | Day 4–7 | 1-week extension for annual plan |

**Target:** 15–20% trial-to-paid. M3 paid target: 40–50 subscribers. MRR: $1,200–$1,500.

### Stage 7 — RETENTION + REFERRAL

| Lever | Detail | Target |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Monthly value email | "Here's what [intern] did this month" digest | <5% churn/mo |
| Referral prompt | In-app banner after 7 days paid | 5% referral rate |
| Commission | 20% recurring ($6/mo per $29.99 referral) | 10 active referrers by M3 |
| Discord community | Showcase wins, feature requests, early access | 80% retention signal |

## 5. Conversion Levers

| Lever | Mechanism | Implementation |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| Trial urgency | 3-day countdown creates natural deadline | Progress bar in app header: "Day 2 of 3" |
| Personality hook | Users emotionally attach to named interns | Every intern has name, avatar, backstory |
| Social proof | Testimonials + user count reduces risk | Homepage: "X teams hired their first intern this week" |
| Zero-friction trial | No CC removes #1 trial barrier | Email-only signup, trial live in <60 sec |
| Referral loop | 20% commission turns users into salespeople | In-app referral link after 7 days paid |
| Retargeting email | Catch drop-offs after trial | Day 4–7 winback sequence (see Section 6) |
| Annual plan hook | Extended access for wavering converters | "Get 2 months free — $299.99/year" offer at expiry |

## 9. Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Countermeasure |
|------|------------|--------|---------------|
| Low Product Hunt day performance (<100 upvotes) | Medium | High | Pre-recruit 50 supporters before launch. Have Discord community ready to upvote. Cross-post to Twitter + Reddit on launch day. Backup: relaunch 30 days later. |
| High trial abandonment (activation <30%) | Medium | High | Reduce friction in first-task flow. Add guided walkthrough. D0 email includes 60-second demo video. Monitor where users drop during onboarding. |
| Trial→paid CVR <10% | Medium | High | Add 1-week extension offer at Day 3. Survey dropoffs ("What stopped you?"). Test pricing page variants. Consider $19.99/mo entry tier or annual plan discount. |
